corrugated_normal.png: tileable normal map for drawing breathing-circuit limbs as tubes (generated by tools/props/airway/textures.py).

Tile length: 36 mm along the tube = 8 corrugations (pitch 4.5 mm). Nothing varies around the tube.
Mapping: U runs around the tube (0..1 once round, any repeat), V runs along the tube; set V = distance_along_tube_mm / 36.
Picture: 512 x 512 px, tangent space, OpenGL / glTF convention (+Y of the picture = +V), linear data (not sRGB).
For a 22 mm adult limb draw the tube at about 25.5 mm outside diameter (crest); normalScale 1.0 gives ~0.7 mm apparent rib depth.
Pitch reference: adult 22 mm corrugated limbs have ribs about 4 to 5 mm apart (product photographs; not a standard).
