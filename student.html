3 · Preop interview

Ask out loud or type. Pick who you are talking to: the patient, or a parent, guardian, or healthcare POA when the patient cannot answer for themself. Everyone only knows what is in the chart. Ask at least three questions before moving on; confirm what the chart left unclear.
