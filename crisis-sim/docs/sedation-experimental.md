# Sedation on a natural airway: the experimental model

Model `experimental-0.2 (2026-11-14)`, in `sim/sedationExperimental.js`. Generated by `node tools/sedation_params_doc.mjs`; do not edit by hand.

**Status: experimental.** Off unless a caller switches it on (`SimRunner.setSedationModel('experimental')`). With it off the engine is
exactly as before (the whole suite passes unchanged). Wherever it runs, the room must say "experimental, not reviewed".

Five kinds of evidence are kept apart:

1. **Source checks** (a citation exists for the value or the formula): 1 parameters.
2. **Source-range** (a value chosen inside a verified published range): 1 parameters.
3. **Owner sign-off** (a decision recorded by the owner): 1 parameter.
4. **Engineering** (a numerical or stability limit, not a physiological claim): 2 parameters.
5. **Unsourced** (a working value chosen so the model moves the right way; must be reviewed before it is taught): 24 parameters.

Software tests (`test/sedation-experimental.test.js`) only show that the model behaves as written and gives the same course from
the same seed. They are not evidence that it is clinically right. **No faculty review has been done.**

## The five pieces of state

| State | What it is | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Depth | BIS from the engine's Eleveld 2018 PK/PD with opioid synergy | existing engine (owner sign-off) |
| Effort | central drive = drug depression (may reach zero) x complication drive x CO2 term | engine coefficients (unsourced, ported) + CO2 slope chosen inside a verified published range |
| Patency | upper airway from depth and collapsibility, relieved by chin lift, jaw thrust, adjuncts | BIS-logistic form retained; current BIS shifts and relief percentages are unsourced |
| Effective ventilation | (tidal volume - dead space) x rate x patency, on ideal body weight | ideal-weight implementation with unsourced operational constants in this table |
| Supplemental oxygen | none, nasal cannula by flow, or the anesthesia mask on the circuit | nasal cannula slope sourced from O'Reilly Nugent 2014 |

The engine's laryngospasm thresholds (patency 0.35 / 0.05; propofol Ce over 1.5 or succinylcholine to break it) belong to the
laryngospasm model and are **not** used here. The engine's own complication patency still multiplies this one.

## Source checks
| Parameter | Value | Source and how it is applied |
|---|---|---|
| `cannulaFio2` | 0.21 + 0.038 per L/min | O'Reilly Nugent 2014 measured about +0.038 FiO2 per L/min by nasal cannula, with 0.296 at 2 L/min, and reported that measured FiO2 falls as minute ventilation rises; that ventilation dependence is not yet modeled here (DOI 10.1111/resp.12268). |

## Source-range
| Parameter | Value | Verified range and chosen value |
|---|---|---|
| `co2SlopeLMinPerMmHg` | 2 | Verified published range for awake CO2 response slope: 1.27 +/- 0.31 L/min/mmHg in steady-state normoxia (Xie 2006, DOI 10.1113/jphysiol.2006.110627) to 2.48 +/- 1.05 L/min/mmHg with rebreathing before epidural fentanyl (Negre 1987, PMID 3111304); 2.0 is chosen inside that range. |

## Engineering
| Parameter | Value | Why this is a numerical/model limit |
|---|---|---|
| `co2DriveMax` | 2.5 | Numerical stability ceiling only. A 2.5x physiological ceiling is not supported by the evidence; healthy adults can exceed it under hypercapnia. This clamp exists only to keep the simulation stable. |
| `cannulaMaxFlow` | 6 | Engineering limit for ordinary nasal cannula flow in this UI/model path; not a physiological claim. |

## Reproducible cases (`test/sedation-experimental.test.js`)
- **A, stable:** 70 kg adult, left lateral, cannula 2 L/min, propofol 40 mg then 300 mg/h for 20 min: breathing kept, patency over 0.85, SpO2 95 % or more, PaCO2 under 55.
- **B, deterioration:** the endoscopy patient (139.4 kg, 178 cm, OSA, BMI 44, Mallampati III), left lateral, cannula 2 L/min, propofol 100 mg + fentanyl 50 mcg + propofol 450 mg/h: patency under 0.4, PaCO2 over 48 and SpO2 under 90 % within 5 min.
- **C, response:** B, answered at the first SpO2 under 92 % with a jaw thrust, the infusion stopped and the cannula at 6 L/min: patency over 0.7, SpO2 at least 94 % at 10 min and not under 92 % after the first two minutes.

## Known limits (found while building it)
- **SpO2 below 90 %.** The engine has no blood oxygen store and no circulation delay, so once saturation passes 90 % it can fall to the floor (1 %) within a minute. The signed-off desaturation work fixed the time to 90 %, not the shape below it. Not changed here.
- **Metabolic rate in obesity.** The engine scales VO2 and VCO2 with total weight. This mode uses an adjusted weight (unsourced for metabolism).
- **Apnea times are kept close to the default engine** (time from apnea to SpO2 90 %, seed 4200): 70 kg preoxygenated 485 s default vs 472 s here; room air 36 s vs 49 s; 139 kg preoxygenated 164 s vs 205 s; room air 16 s vs 19 s. The 139 kg difference comes from the metabolic weight.
- Not modeled: gagging or coughing with an oral airway in a lighter patient, mouth breathing on a cannula, the endoscope's own stimulation of the airway, capnography sampling dilution under a cannula.
