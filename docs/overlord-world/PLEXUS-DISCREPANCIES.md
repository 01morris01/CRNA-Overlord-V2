# The plexus model: what disagreed with the textbooks, and what was done

The first audit of the brachial plexus model (23 checks) reported five failures, and at that time the lesson was reworded
around them. That was the wrong response. Anatomy is not changed to suit an asset. This is the record of going back to
each one: what the textbook says, what the model showed, why they differed, and what was corrected.

Method. `tools/audit_plexus.py` was rewritten so that every check is a statement read in context on a printed page
(Hadzic 2e ch. 3 pp. 43-52; Nagelhout 7e ch. 50 pp. 1149-1156), measured on the model's geometry. It has 48 checks. The
unchanged source model passes 19 of the 30 that can be evaluated on it (`plexus-audit-source.json`). The corrected model
passes 48 of 48 (`plexus-audit.json`). Corrections are generated by `tools/plexus_corrections_plan.py`
(`plexus-corrections-plan.json`), and every corrected or newly built structure is tagged in the model file and labelled
in the app ("Corrected from the source model", "Built for Overlord").

All of this was done by an AI assistant. No anatomist or faculty member has reviewed the corrected model.

## The five original failures

| # | Check that failed | What the source says (paraphrased, with page) | What the model showed | Cause | Classification | What was done |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Superior trunk lies in the interscalene groove | The rami pass between the anterior and middle scalenes and join into trunks at the muscles' lateral border (Hadzic p. 44; Nagelhout p. 1149) | The trunk began 19 mm from the anterior scalene and 8 mm from the middle scalene: behind the interval, not in it | The trunk curve's medial end was drawn about 17 mm posterior to the interval, and the model had no root objects at all | **Incorrect model** (misplaced origin; missing roots). The first audit's wording was also loose: it tested the whole trunk for lying "inside the groove", which is the roots' relationship, not the trunks' | Trunk origin moved 16.7 mm into the measured interval (5.1 mm wide there). Roots C5 and C6 built from their foramina through the interval to the trunk. Check rewritten from the source statement |
| 2 | Middle trunk lies in the interscalene groove | Same statement | Began 30 mm from the anterior scalene, 16 mm from the middle | Same; origin about 27 mm posterior | **Incorrect model** | Origin moved 27.5 mm. Root C7 built |
| 3 | Inferior trunk lies in the interscalene groove | Same statement | Began 19 mm from the anterior scalene, 6 mm from the middle | Same; origin about 16 mm posterior | **Incorrect model** | Origin moved 16.0 mm. Roots C8 and T1 built |
| 4 | Musculocutaneous nerve meets coracobrachialis | It is the continuation of the lateral cord (Nagelhout p. 1149) and pierces coracobrachialis (Hadzic p. 50) | No point of the nerve was inside the muscle (closest 8.5 mm). The curve began in the arm, 117 mm below the trunks, joined to nothing | The source model has no lateral cord, and its musculocutaneous curve is a detached fragment beside the muscle | **Incorrect model** (incomplete and detached) | Lateral cord built. The nerve re-attached to it and routed through the muscle; the check now tests containment in the closed muscle mesh |
| 5 | Posterior cord joins all three posterior divisions and gives the axillary and radial nerves | All three posterior divisions form the posterior cord (Hadzic p. 44); the axillary and radial nerves arise from it (Nagelhout p. 1149; Hadzic p. 48) | The inferior trunk's posterior division ended 11 mm from the posterior cord, at the junction the lateral structures share. The axillary nerve began 7 mm from the cord, at that same wrong junction | Attachment errors where the model's curves meet: a topology mistake, not a variant (the median nerve also began on the posterior cord, and two nerves carried stray one-point splines) | **Incorrect model** (wrong connections) | That division re-attached to the posterior cord; the axillary nerve re-attached to it; the median nerve re-formed from a lateral and a medial root; stray splines removed |

None of the five is an anatomical variant, and none is a labelling error in the sense of a correct shape with a wrong
name. Failures 1 to 3 include a fault in the first audit's own assumption, stated above.

## Structures the source model lacked

Built from the model's own landmarks to match the cited descriptions, and verified by the audit: roots C5 to T1 (10
checks), the lateral and medial cords, the lateral and medial roots of the median nerve, and the cervical part of the
phrenic nerve on the anterior scalene (Hadzic p. 43). Still not modelled, and never asked for: the phrenic nerve above and
below the neck, C3 and C4, the intercostobrachial nerve.

## Four measurements that were changed after the strict audit first ran

In each case the first version of the measurement did not measure what the source sentence says. The pass thresholds were
not loosened; the thing measured was corrected. They are listed because a changed check should never be silent.

| Check | First measurement | Why it did not measure the statement | Measurement now |
|---|---|---|---|
| A root leaves the spine at its own level | Compared the root's start with the vertebral body's bounding box | A foramen lies between two adjacent bodies, not level with one | The root's start must lie between the centres of the two adjacent vertebral bodies |
| Roots join to form trunks | Required the root's end vertex to coincide with the trunk's first vertex | Tubes meet end to end without sharing a vertex | End-to-end distance between the two tubes |
| Trunks and divisions pass over the first rib | Nearest distance to the rib in 3D | "Over" is a statement about plan position and height | Rib directly beneath in plan view, and the plexus above it |
| Axillary nerve passes behind the surgical neck | Compared with the humerus's overall centre | The humerus is not vertical, so its centre is not "the bone" at the neck's height | Compared with the bone's axis at the same height |

## What is excluded from scored teaching

Anything without both a page-level source and a passing geometric check. See `CONTENT-VERIFICATION.md` for the claims that
were looked for and not found, and were removed.
