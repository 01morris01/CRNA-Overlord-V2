# Overlord identity

One idea, used sparingly: **a form cut by a section plane, with the part below the plane shifted a little along it.**
It comes from the thing the product is about (looking inside by cutting), not from a theme. It is drawn, not described:
`world/js/brand.js` generates every mark, and the files in `world/brand/` are its output.

| Element | What it is | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | OVERLORD in monoline capitals built on a 40-unit cap height, cut once at one height, the lower part shifted 3 units, with a hairline along the cut (`wordmark.svg`, `wordmark-dark.svg`) | The sign-in card, the loading screen, the menu button, and in brass on the entrance façade |
| Mark | The wordmark's O on its own (`mark.svg`) | The menu button; the sleeve patch; plates |
| Datum line | A thin horizontal line, the cut made architectural | The brass line round the court at 1.1 m; the two brass inlays in the floor from the door to the gallery; the short coral rule under every heading, offset 3 px like the cut |
| Section ring | A thin brass ring that marks a cutting plane | The atrium figure (you move it); the brass rings set in the floor where something can be opened; the coral frame of the cut in the scan exhibit |
| Exhibit frame | Black panel, thin brass edge, a brass strip under a pale stone top | Plinths, the plexus backdrop, the lightbox, the mirror, the collection wall |
| Wayfinding | Small brass plates, engraved capitals over a hairline | Beside every doorway and on every mount |
| Transition | A coral hairline crosses the screen and what is behind it has changed | Walking to examining and back |
| Patch | The mark in a rounded field; one short coral tick per completed series (`patch.svg`) | The character card; a small frame on the studio shelf |

**Color.** Stone `#EAE6DF`, concrete `#C9C5BD`, charcoal `#16181B`, ink `#0B0C0E`, paper `#F4F4F2`, brass `#C89B55`,
bone `#DDD3BD`, and one signal color, coral `#FF5A3C`, which always means alive, active or yours.

**Type.** Inter Tight, light for headings, regular for text, medium for labels.

**Restraint.** The identity is a line and a ring. Rooms are not themed. With the wordmark, plates and every overlay
hidden, the building is still stone, concrete, timber, brass edges and daylight.

**Not used.** Victorian theatre, curtains, playbills, menace, retro terminals, neon dashboards, pixel or block graphics.
